Customer Churn Analysis & Retention Strategy:

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn behavior to identify key drivers of customer attrition and provide actionable
business recommendations to improve retention.
Using a combination of data analysis, visualization, and business storytelling, the project uncovers patterns across
customer demographics, contract types, tenure, and payment methods.

Key Highlights:
Analyzed customer data to identify high-risk churn segments
Discovered that short-tenure and month-to-month contract customers show significantly higher churn rates
Identified impact of factors like payment method, service usage, and customer tenure on retention.

Business Insights:
Customers with shorter tenure are more likely to churn → Need early engagement strategies
Month-to-month contracts have higher churn → Opportunity to promote long-term plans
Specific customer segments can be targeted with personalized retention offers.

Recommendations:
Introduce loyalty programs for new customers
Offer incentives to shift customers to long-term contracts
Focus on high-risk segments with proactive retention campaigns.

Tech Stack:
Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Machine Learning(Random Forest))
Power BI (Interactive Dashboard, DAX)
Data Cleaning & Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
SQL Server Management Studio (SQL Queries) 

Project Objective:

To transform raw customer data into meaningful insights and help businesses make data-driven decisions to reduce churn
and improve customer lifetime value.
